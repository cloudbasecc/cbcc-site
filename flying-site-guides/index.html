Flying Site Guides

The state of Washington offers a wide variety of opportunities for hang glider and paraglider pilots of all skill levels, from gentle consistent ridge soaring to world-class cross-country flying.

Geographically, the state is divided into two major regions by the Cascade mountains. To the west (“Western Washington”), the climate is dominated by marine influences from the Pacific Ocean and Puget Sound – mild and wet, giving rise to gentle thermals, relatively low cloudbase, and large forested areas.

Washington Flying Sites Managed by CBCC

  • Dog Mountain
    CBCC's “home site” near Morton – consistent ridge soaring year-round.
  • Saddle Mountain
    A desert ridge-soaring site near Mattawa.

Other Washington Flying Sites
