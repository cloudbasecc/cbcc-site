Membership

Annual membership in CBCC is $25 for Saddle-only membership, or $100 if you also fly and support Dog Mt. All memberships run from June 1 to May 31. Use the form below to register for membership or pay your annual dues through PayPal:

Download the Dog Mountain Waiver (2025)

The club’s “home site” is Dog Mountain, so as part of joining the club (or maintaining your membership), we ask that you download, print, and sign the Dog Mountain waiver annually before flying the site.

Dog Mountain gate keys are primarily distributed either at Frostbite or a “key day” (see the Calendar). This is a time-consuming process, and we ask for your help in keeping our volunteers sane by taking advantage of the main distribution day. Alternatively, arrangements can be made to pick one up in person from one of the Dog site administrators. We do NOT mail keys.

Our club dues pay for annual USHPA site insurance and land use fees at Dog and Saddle, incidental club operation costs such as Dog Port-a-Potty cleaning, this website, occasional club parties, and anything else the membership votes to support. Thanks for being a member!

Cloudbase Country Club (CBCC) is a member club of the United States Hang Gliding and Paragliding Association.
